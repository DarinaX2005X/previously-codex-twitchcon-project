## The family (applies to all six)

These are hero objects for slides about Previously, a tool that gives Twitch raiders and late viewers a recap of the stream. They sit on saturated colour fields next to large native text, like the 3D objects in premium Russian bank and product decks (Ozon, Sber): one large, crafted object per slide.

- Material: smooth matte soft-touch plastic / ceramic, gently rounded bevels, visible thickness, no texture noise, no cheap gloss. One small accent may be clear glass or a soft iridescent sheen.
- Palette, only these: vermilion #FF5A36 (darker side #C93F22), warm paper cream #F4EFE6 (side #CFC5B3), ink #14171B, lime #C8F560 (side #86A833).
- Light: one key light from the upper left, soft fill, a gentle rim on the right edge. A soft contact shadow directly under the object is fine; nothing else on the background.
- Camera: three-quarter view from slightly above, about 25° to the right, slightly wide lens. Same camera for all six.
- Strictly no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no faces, no people, no particles, no sparkles, no confetti, no extra props.

## The six objects (file names)

1. `bubble.png`: a large chat speech bubble in lime matte material, thick like a cushion (depth about a quarter of its width), rounded rectangle with a short tail at the lower left. On its front face a question mark is debossed (pressed in), the same lime with a darker inner shading. Behind it, overlapping, one smaller speech bubble in cream, slightly turned.
2. `keycap.png`: one oversized mechanical keyboard keycap in lime matte plastic, sculpted top with a gentle concave dish, visible stem underneath. The legend on top is an exclamation mark, raised and in ink colour. Only this one key.
3. `hourglass.png`: an hourglass with a vermilion matte frame (two round discs top and bottom, three slim pillars), glass bulbs, warm cream sand: most of it still in the top bulb, a thin stream falling, a small heap below.
4. `eraser.png`: a chunky block eraser lying at a slight angle, vermilion top half and cream bottom half like a classic two-tone eraser, one end worn into a soft curve, three or four small cream eraser crumbs beside it.
5. `modules.png`: four rounded square matte modules (two cream, one vermilion, one lime) slotted side by side into a single ink-coloured base tray, each module a slightly different height, like building blocks plugged into one board. Seen from the same three-quarter camera.
6. `bubbles-stack.png`: five matte speech bubbles of different sizes stacked and overlapping in a loose tower, alternating vermilion, cream, lime, ink and cream, each a cushion-thick rounded rectangle with a small tail, all blank.

Keep every object fully inside the frame with some empty margin. Prefer one excellent object over decoration.
